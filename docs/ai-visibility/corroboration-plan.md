## 2. What counts (and what does not)
- **Independent:** a journalist's article, a partner developer or broker page written by them, a directory listing they host, a genuine customer review on a third-party platform.
- **Not independent:** our own pages, our social posts, paid advertorials, press releases republished unchanged. Paid placements are labelled as such in our tracking and never counted as corroboration.
