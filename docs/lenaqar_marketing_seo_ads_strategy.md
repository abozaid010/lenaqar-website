## 1. Executive Summary

Lenaqar is repositioning from a property-listing/seller-led proposition to a **buyer-first real estate platform built around property requests**.

Traditional portals primarily ask buyers to browse properties that someone else has chosen to advertise. Lenaqar starts with the buyer: the buyer describes what they need, where they want it, their budget, and their requirements. Lenaqar then helps identify properties that fit those needs.

### Core positioning

**English:** We don't sell you what we have. We find you what you want.

**Egyptian Arabic:** إحنا مش بنبيعلك اللي عندنا.. بنلاقي لك اللي إنت عايزه.

### Supporting promise

**Arabic:** اكتب طلبك زي ما إنت عايز، وإحنا نساعدك تلاقي العقار المناسب لاحتياجك وميزانيتك.

**English:** Tell us what you want. We'll help you find a property that fits your needs and budget.

### Strategic priorities

1. Make the buyer the primary audience across the website and marketing.
2. Explain the request-first model clearly and quickly.
3. Reduce buyer anxiety around irrelevant sales calls and sales-driven recommendations.
4. Attract high-intent buyers through SEO and paid search.
5. Turn buyer requests into qualified leads and suitable property matches.
6. Keep sellers and brokers as supporting participants who can respond to genuine demand.
7. Build trust through accurate property information, clear processes, privacy, and transparent commercial practices.
8. Measure success by qualified buyer requests, useful matches, viewings, and completed transactions—not traffic alone.

## 2. Brand Positioning

### 2.1 What Lenaqar is

Lenaqar is a buyer-first real estate platform where people can describe the home they want instead of relying only on browsing conventional property listings.

A buyer might write:

- “I want a two-bedroom apartment in New Cairo, with a budget of EGP 5 million, ready to move in.”
- “I need a four-bedroom chalet on the North Coast, budget EGP 6 million, beachfront.”
- “I want a villa in Sheikh Zayed, within my budget, and I prefer a completed property.”
- “I am looking for a property with specific payment terms and a particular delivery date.”

Lenaqar helps the buyer communicate those requirements and find suitable options from the available property supply and participating real estate professionals.

### 2.2 What Lenaqar is not

- Not merely another portal of property advertisements.
- Not a platform whose primary objective is to push whatever inventory is currently available.
- Not a promise that every request will always have an exact match.
- Not a guarantee of the lowest market price unless that guarantee is formally defined and supported.
- Not a claim that every property is completed, verified, or directly owned by Lenaqar.

### 2.3 Positioning statement

For people in Egypt who want to buy a home, Lenaqar is a buyer-first real estate platform that starts with their needs, budget, location, and preferences. Instead of asking buyers to search endlessly through listings, Lenaqar helps them find properties that match their requirements.

### 2.4 Value proposition

**Functional value**
- Buyers can describe what they want in their own words.
- Requirements can include area, budget, property type, bedrooms, delivery status, view, payment terms, and other constraints.
- The platform can help connect demand with suitable property supply.
- Buyers can focus on options that fit their needs rather than browsing irrelevant inventory.

**Emotional value**
- More control over the buying journey.
- Less pressure to consider unsuitable properties.
- Greater confidence that their requirements have been heard.
- A more understandable way to begin a high-value purchase.

**Commercial value**
- Better-qualified demand for brokers and property owners.
- More relevant introductions between buyers and available properties.
- Less wasted effort on properties that do not meet the buyer's stated requirements.

Only promise functionality that is actually available and working in production.

## 3. Target Audiences

### 3.1 Primary audience: residential property buyers

Potential segments:

1. **First-time homebuyers** who need guidance and clarity.
2. **Families upgrading their home** who have specific bedroom, location, and lifestyle requirements.
3. **Buyers with a fixed budget** who need help narrowing down realistic options.
4. **Ready-to-move buyers** who prefer completed or immediately available properties.
5. **Coastal-property buyers** looking for chalets, beachfront access, or specific North Coast areas.
6. **Investors** who have defined location, budget, rental, resale, or delivery criteria.
7. **Buyers frustrated by sales calls** who prefer to state their requirements before discussing options.
8. **Buyers seeking resale properties** who want to consider existing homes as well as new launches.

Do not assume every segment has equal value. Use actual qualified-lead and transaction data to prioritize.

### 3.2 Secondary audience: property owners and sellers

Their role is to supply properties that may fit buyer requests.

Message:
**عندك وحدة تناسب طلب مشتري حقيقي؟ ابعتهالنا.**

English:
Have a property that matches a real buyer's requirements? Share it with us.

### 3.3 Secondary audience: brokers and real estate professionals

Their role is to help fulfill relevant buyer demand by providing suitable and accurate property options.

Message:
**طلبات شراء واضحة تساعدك توصل لوحدات مناسبة لعملائك.**

Do not position the platform primarily as a broker CRM or seller-advertising portal in buyer-facing messaging.

## 5. Claims, Trust & Compliance

Marketing must be persuasive without making claims that cannot be demonstrated.

### 5.1 Claims requiring evidence

Treat the following as unverified until supported:

- “The first buyer-first platform in Egypt.”
- “No cold calls.”
- “We never recommend properties based on commission.”
- “Best price” or “lowest price.”
- “Verified properties.”
- “Guaranteed match.”
- “Guaranteed sale in a specified number of days.”
- “Every property is ready to move.”
- Any numerical claim about users, requests, properties, success rate, or time saved.

### 5.2 Safer alternatives

Instead of **“The first platform in Egypt”**, use:
- “منصة عقارية بتبدأ من طلب المشتري.”
- “طريقة مختلفة للبحث عن عقارك.”
- “منصة بتساعدك تلاقي عقار مناسب لاحتياجك.”

Instead of **“We never call you”**, use a statement that matches the actual consent and follow-up policy, such as:
- “ابدأ بطلبك، وحدد طريقة التواصل المناسبة ليك.”
- “معلومات أوضح وطلبات أكثر تحديدًا من البداية.”

Instead of **“We always find exactly what you want”**, use:
- “هنساعدك تدور على اختيارات مناسبة لطلبك.”
- “كل ما كان طلبك أوضح، كان أسهل نحدد الاختيارات المناسبة.”

### 5.3 Trust requirements

- Do not expose buyer names, phone numbers, or private details on public request pages.
- Make request status and availability clear.
- Remove or mark expired requests.
- Avoid showing misleading prices, delivery dates, or payment figures.
- Clearly distinguish completed properties, resale units, under-construction units, and new launches.
- Explain how buyer contact details are used.
- Disclose material commercial relationships when appropriate.
- Ensure ads and landing pages describe the actual product experience.

## 7. SEO Strategy

### 7.1 SEO objective

Attract people who are actively researching or planning to buy property in Egypt, then help them submit a useful request.

SEO should support three stages:

- **Discovery:** Learn about areas, prices, property types, and buying options.
- **Consideration:** Compare areas and understand resale, new launches, delivery, and budgets.
- **Action:** Submit a buyer request or explore suitable, current requests.

### 7.4 Landing-page content template

Each location/property landing page should include:

1. Unique SEO title and meta description.
2. One clear H1 matching the page intent.
3. A concise, original introduction about the buyer's use case and area.
4. Current, useful requests or matching property information.
5. Filters relevant to that category.
6. Clear labels for price, availability, delivery status, and payment terms.
7. An explanation of how Lenaqar helps buyers.
8. Primary CTA to submit a request.
9. Internal links to closely related areas and property types.
10. Relevant FAQs based on actual buyer questions.
11. Appropriate structured data only where it reflects the content.
12. Useful empty states if no matching requests are currently available.

### 7.5 Avoid thin or duplicate SEO pages

Do not index every combination of:
- Budget.
- Bedroom count.
- Location.
- Property type.
- Delivery date.
- View or amenity.
- Sorting and filter parameters.

Create a crawlable indexable page only when it has distinct search demand and enough unique, useful content. Keep low-value combinations out of the index or use suitable canonical/noindex handling based on a technical audit.

### 7.6 Request-page indexing policy

A public buyer request can be useful content, but not every request should become an indexed page.

Index a request only if it:
- Has public-display permission.
- Contains useful, non-sensitive information.
- Is current and not expired.
- Offers distinct value to searchers.
- Does not reveal personal information.
- Is rendered and crawlable.
- Has a stable canonical URL and a clear status.

Otherwise, keep it out of the sitemap and search index. When requests expire, update the page, remove it from the index where appropriate, and avoid leaving misleading stale content.

### 7.7 Technical SEO checklist

- [ ] Choose one canonical host: `https://lenaqar.com/` or `https://www.lenaqar.com/`.
- [ ] Redirect the alternate host and HTTP URLs to the canonical version.
- [ ] Audit existing URLs before changing paths.
- [ ] Verify title tags and meta descriptions on key pages.
- [ ] Ensure each page has a meaningful H1.
- [ ] Confirm important content renders for search crawlers.
- [ ] Check robots.txt and meta robots directives.
- [ ] Generate a sitemap containing canonical, indexable URLs only.
- [ ] Remove private pages, duplicates, expired requests, and irrelevant filter URLs from the sitemap.
- [ ] Use correct canonical tags.
- [ ] Implement `hreflang` only if separate Arabic/English versions exist and are correctly paired.
- [ ] Use `Organization`, `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList`, and other schema only when accurate.
- [ ] Do not mark buyer requests as property sale offers if they are not property listings.
- [ ] Validate structured data.
- [ ] Check mobile usability and Core Web Vitals.
- [ ] Optimize images and meaningful alt text.
- [ ] Fix broken links, redirect chains, 404s, and server errors.
- [ ] Ensure no buyer phone numbers, names, or private data leak into HTML, schema, metadata, or public APIs.
- [ ] Verify Search Console ownership and sitemap submission.
- [ ] Check indexing and crawl errors after release.

### 7.8 Local SEO and geographic coverage

Prioritize locations based on:
1. Actual buyer requests.
2. Available matching inventory.
3. Qualified-lead and transaction history.
4. Search demand.
5. Ability to provide accurate, original information.

Do not create location pages for places Lenaqar cannot meaningfully serve. Avoid generic pages with only the area name changed.

### 7.9 Internal linking

Link between:
- Area pages and property-type pages.
- Area pages and useful buyer guides.
- Buyer guides and relevant request forms.
- Public requests and the broader area/property category, where appropriate.
- Related locations when the comparison helps buyers.

Use descriptive anchor text naturally. Do not stuff every link with the same exact-match keyword.

## 8. Content Marketing Plan

### 8.1 Content principles

- Write for real buyer questions, not search engines alone.
- Use Egyptian Arabic when it matches the intended audience and brand voice.
- Add real examples, practical comparisons, and transparent limitations.
- Do not publish fabricated prices, property availability, statistics, or claims.
- Review content whenever market conditions or product features change.
- Give each article a purpose and a next step.
- Prefer a few useful, accurate pieces over many generic AI-generated articles.

### 8.3 Four-week launch plan

**Week 1 — Foundation**
- Audit existing site, URLs, metadata, indexability, analytics, and conversion events.
- Finalize homepage messaging.
- Fix incorrect or stale request data.
- Set up baseline reporting.

**Week 2 — High-intent landing pages**
- Publish or improve the New Cairo apartment page.
- Publish or improve the North Coast chalet page if current data supports it.
- Add internal links and request CTAs.

**Week 3 — Buyer-specific pages**
- Create ready-to-move and relevant budget/area pages only where they provide unique value.
- Publish two buyer guides.
- Review request-page privacy and expiry handling.

**Week 4 — Learn and optimize**
- Review Search Console queries and page performance.
- Compare conversion rates by landing page and channel.
- Improve pages with impressions but weak click-through rate.
- Improve high-traffic pages with weak request submission.
- Plan the next month based on evidence.

## 9. Paid Advertising Strategy

SEO builds durable visibility; paid advertising can test demand and generate buyer requests sooner. Treat ad budgets as experiments until cost per qualified request and downstream transaction economics are known.

### 9.1 Campaign objectives

Primary conversion:
- A successfully submitted buyer request that meets minimum quality requirements.

Secondary conversions:
- Started but not completed request.
- Click to WhatsApp.
- Contact or consultation request.
- Engagement with a relevant area page.

Do not optimize only for clicks, page views, or cheap unqualified leads.

### 9.2 Google Search Ads

Start with high-intent search themes such as:
- شقق للبيع في القاهرة الجديدة
- شقق استلام فوري في القاهرة الجديدة
- شاليهات للبيع في الساحل الشمالي
- فلل للبيع في الشيخ زايد

Use separate ad groups and relevant landing pages. Do not send every ad to the homepage if a more relevant page exists.

**Example ad copy — New Cairo**

Headline ideas:
- بتدور على شقة في القاهرة الجديدة؟
- اكتب طلبك العقاري
- اختار حسب ميزانيتك واحتياجك
- دور على شقة تناسبك

Description:
`حدد ميزانيتك ومواصفات الشقة اللي محتاجها. ابدأ بطلبك على لينا عقار وشوف اختيارات مناسبة لاحتياجك.`

**Example ad copy — North Coast**

Headline ideas:
- بتدور على شاليه في الساحل؟
- اكتب مواصفات الشاليه اللي عايزه
- اختار حسب ميزانيتك ومكانك المفضل

Description:
`حدد ميزانيتك وعدد الغرف والمكان المفضل. ابدأ بطلبك واكتشف الخيارات المتاحة المناسبة لمواصفاتك.`

Only use urgency, availability, exact prices, or claims about matching if they are accurate and up to date.

### 9.3 Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram)

Use Meta to introduce the buyer-first idea and retarget eligible visitors where consent and platform policies allow.

Creative concepts:
1. **Problem/solution:** endless listings versus writing one clear request.
2. **Request example:** a sample request such as “شقة غرفتين في القاهرة الجديدة بميزانية 5 مليون واستلام فوري.”
3. **Buyer control:** show the steps from requirements to relevant options.
4. **Property-specific:** apartment, villa, or chalet creatives tied to a relevant landing page.
5. **Trust:** explain what information is requested and how privacy is handled.

Example primary text:
`بدل ما تلف على إعلانات كتير، قولنا إنت محتاج إيه. حدد المنطقة والميزانية والمواصفات، وابدأ طلبك على لينا عقار.`

Example headline:
`بيتك يبدأ من طلبك`

CTA:
`Learn More` or the closest supported action that leads to the request flow.

Do not imply knowledge of sensitive personal circumstances. Keep ad targeting and copy compliant with platform policies.

### 9.4 Retargeting

Potential audiences, subject to consent, platform policy, and applicable law:
- Visitors to relevant area pages.
- Visitors who started but did not submit a request.
- Users who engaged with relevant video or social content.

Use frequency caps where available and avoid excessive follow-up. Exclude users who have already submitted a request from acquisition campaigns when appropriate.

### 9.5 Campaign structure

Keep campaigns separated enough to identify performance:
- Search — New Cairo apartments.
- Search — North Coast chalets.
- Search — other validated property categories.
- Meta — buyer-first concept testing.
- Meta — relevant location/property creative.
- Retargeting — incomplete request or engaged visitor, where permitted.

Avoid launching many campaigns with insufficient budget to learn.

### 9.6 Ad landing-page requirements

- The page matches the ad's location and property type.
- The request CTA is visible without excessive scrolling.
- The form is mobile-friendly.
- The page explains what happens after submission.
- The form does not ask for unnecessary information.
- Privacy and contact expectations are clear.
- Tracking records submission success, not just button clicks.
- No false availability or unsubstantiated “guaranteed match” claim.

### 9.7 Advertising experiments

For each experiment record:
- Hypothesis.
- Audience and geography.
- Campaign and creative.
- Landing page.
- Budget and duration.
- Primary conversion.
- Number of submitted requests.
- Qualified requests.
- Cost per qualified request.
- Viewings and completed transactions, when measurable.
- Decision: continue, revise, or stop.

Do not declare a winner from a small sample without noting uncertainty.

## 10. Lead Quality and Conversion Funnel

### 10.1 Funnel

1. Search/social impression.
2. Visit to relevant page.
3. Start request.
4. Submit request.
5. Request meets quality criteria.
6. Relevant property match identified.
7. Buyer reviews a match.
8. Contact or viewing.
9. Offer/negotiation.
10. Completed transaction.

### 10.2 Define a qualified request

Create a consistent internal definition. A starting point might require:
- Valid contact method and permission to use it.
- Realistic, sufficiently clear location and property criteria.
- Budget or a clear indication that the budget is still being determined.
- A genuine intent to buy within a defined timeframe, where collected.
- No duplicate or spam submission.

Do not reject buyers just because their request does not immediately match current inventory; track unmatched demand separately.

### 10.3 Track unmatched demand

Unmatched requests are valuable research. Categorize them by:
- Area.
- Property type.
- Budget band.
- Bedroom count.
- Delivery requirement.
- Must-have features.
- Reason no match was found.
- Whether the request later received a suitable match.

Use this information to guide supply acquisition, content priorities, and advertising—not just to report failed conversions.

## 13. Brand Voice and Copy Rules

### Voice
- Egyptian Arabic for consumer-facing Arabic copy when natural.
- Direct, clear, practical, and respectful.
- Buyer-focused rather than pushy.
- Helpful without promising unrealistic outcomes.
- Explain property terms in plain language.

### Prefer
- “اكتب طلبك”
- “حسب احتياجك وميزانيتك”
- “اختيارات مناسبة لطلبك”
- “خلينا نساعدك تدور”
- “تفاصيل أوضح قبل ما تقرر”

### Avoid
- Unsupported guarantees.
- Fake urgency.
- “أفضل سعر في مصر” without proof.
- “مضمون 100%” without a clear, enforceable guarantee.
- Claims that every option is verified or available if that is not true.
- Copy that makes the platform sound primarily like a seller's advertising service.

### English-language messaging
- “Start with what you need.”
- “Your requirements come first.”
- “Tell us what you want. Find options that fit.”
- “A property search built around your needs.”
- “Less irrelevant browsing. More relevant options.”

## 21. Guiding Principles

1. **Buyer first:** start with what the buyer needs.
2. **Specific beats generic:** target real areas, budgets, property types, and requirements.
3. **Trust is part of conversion:** accurate information and honest claims matter.
4. **Demand informs supply:** unmatched requests are useful market signals.
5. **Quality beats volume:** a qualified buyer request is more valuable than an irrelevant click.
6. **Measure outcomes:** connect SEO and ads to matches, viewings, and transactions where possible.
7. **Keep promises operationally true:** marketing must match the actual product and process.
8. **Experiment deliberately:** record the hypothesis, result, and decision.
9. **Preserve existing SEO value:** audit before changing URLs or removing pages.
10. **Keep this document alive:** add new ideas, evidence, results, and decisions as Lenaqar develops.
