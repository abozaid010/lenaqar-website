Key findings from the report

Widest, deepest resale market: Madinaty, with roughly 1,930 active apartment listings compound-wide and the broadest range of sizes/prices inside the budget.

Best value-for-money (lowest price/sqm for the largest units): Palm Hills Capital Gardens (Mostakbal City) and City Gate (Qatari Diar), both under EGP 36,000/sqm median versus EGP 49,000-55,000/sqm at Madinaty, Palm Hills New Cairo and ZED East.

Best computed rental yield: Hyde Park New Cairo (~7.6% gross) and Madinaty (~6.6% gross); weakest: Sarai (~3.3%) and Palm Hills New Cairo (~4.3%).

Highest physical/landscape quality on the data gathered: SODIC Villette and ZED East, but both have very thin in-budget supply — a 10M budget buys only the lower end of their inventory.

Overall top-ranked projects for a 3BR EGP 5-10M cash buyer: Madinaty, Mountain View iCity, SODIC Villette (with caveats), Hyde Park New Cairo, ZED East, Palm Hills New Cairo, Sarai, and Taj City.

Quantitative figures are High confidence. Qualitative assessments are Medium confidence. See report Section 8.

Screened but Excluded

Compounds with insufficient 3BR resale supply inside EGP 5,000,000–10,000,000.

SODIC Eastown

1 of 66 scanned listings in budget

Live 3BR resale listings (66 cards scanned across 2 PropertyFinder pages) are overwhelmingly priced EGP 10,350,000–25,000,000+ for 130–278 sqm units; only 1 listing (EGP 7,700,000 / 195 sqm) fell inside the EGP 5–10M budget.

Aqarmap's compound-wide apartment average is 89,650 EGP/sqm.

Mivida (Emaar)

1 of 31 scanned listings in budget

3BR apartments run 190–289 sqm; of 31 live listings scanned, only 1 fell inside EGP 5–10M and the median price across all 3BR listings was approximately EGP 19,000,000.

The Waterway View (90th St)

1 of 31 scanned listings in budget

Only 1 of 31 live 3BR listings fell inside EGP 5–10M (EGP 8,800,000 / 182 sqm); the broader range for the project runs up to EGP 17,000,000.

Swan Lake Residence (Hassan Allam)

1 of 35 scanned listings in budget

Only 1 of 35 live 3BR listings fell inside EGP 5–10M (EGP 8,700,000 / 155 sqm). Insufficient in-budget sample for a full evaluation.
